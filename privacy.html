Privacy Policy

Soul Rush · PL Labs · Last updated August 9, 2026

This policy describes what information Soul Rush, published by PL Labs, handles when you play, and the choices you have. The short version: there is no account system and no login. If you choose to appear on the global leaderboard, a name you type in and your score are made public; the game does not otherwise ask for anything personal.

Information we collect

Soul Rush does not have an account system, a login, or a registration step, and it never asks for your email address or any real-world identity information.

If you choose to submit a run to the global leaderboard, the game sends: the display name you typed in (visible to everyone who views the board), your score and run duration, and a title you've selected. Alongside this, the app generates a random identifier on your device the first time you play — a string it invents itself, not your Android advertising ID, IMEI, or any other real hardware identifier — and uses it, together with a second random secret kept on your device, to prove that later updates to your score come from the same install. Neither value means anything outside Soul Rush, and both reset if you clear the app's storage or reinstall. Using the leaderboard is optional; the game is fully playable without ever submitting a score.

This data is stored on our backend (Supabase) for as long as your leaderboard entry exists, is never sold, and is shared with no one beyond what's needed to display the board itself.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (best scores, streaks, unlocked cosmetics, and settings) is saved in local storage on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

Soul Rush does not currently show ads. When ads are introduced, they will be served by AppLovin, a third-party advertising network, and this page will be updated first. See the AppLovin Privacy Policy for how they handle data.

To serve and measure ads, AppLovin may automatically collect device information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from it, device model, and how you interact with the ads shown. PL Labs does not receive or store this data; it is processed by AppLovin under its own policy linked above.

You can limit ad personalization at any time from your device settings: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization. Where required by law, the game will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you may decline.

Analytics

The game does not use any analytics service beyond the measurement built into the advertising SDK described above.

Children

Soul Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ad network is configured to comply with applicable children's privacy rules on Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or its ads, contact us and we will help address it.

Permissions

The game requests network access only, used to submit and load leaderboard scores and, once introduced, to load ads. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files, or precise location.

Deleting your data

To have your leaderboard entry removed, email us at the address below from any address, telling us the display name shown on the board. We delete the entry and cannot recover it afterward. Progress stored only on your device (scores, streaks, cosmetics, settings) is deleted by uninstalling the game or clearing its data yourself — we have no copy of it to remove.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data changes, for example if an SDK is added or removed, this page will be updated and the date above revised. Material changes will be noted in the game's store listing.
