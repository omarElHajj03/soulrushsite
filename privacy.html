Privacy Policy

Soul Rush · PL Labs · Last updated July 16, 2026

This policy describes what information Soul Rush (“the game”), published by PL Labs, handles when you play, and the choices you have. The short version: the game has no accounts, asks for nothing personal, and keeps your progress on your own device. The only third parties involved are the advertising networks that serve ads in the free version.

Information we collect

Soul Rush itself does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. There is no account system, no login, no registration, and no form that asks for your name, email address, or any other personal detail.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — best scores, streaks, unlocked cosmetics, and settings — is saved in local storage on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by third-party advertising networks:

  • Google AdMob — see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.
  • AppLovin — see the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

To serve and measure ads, these networks may automatically collect device information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from it, device model, and how you interact with the ads shown. PL Labs does not receive or store this data; it is processed by the ad networks under their own policies linked above.

You can limit ad personalization at any time from your device settings: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization. Where required by law, the game will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you may decline.

Analytics

The game does not use any analytics service beyond the measurement built into the advertising SDKs described above.

Children

Soul Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad networks are configured to comply with applicable children's privacy rules on Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or its ads, contact us and we will help address it.

Permissions

The game requests network access only, which is used solely to load ads. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files, or precise location.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data changes — for example, if an SDK is added or removed — this page will be updated and the date above revised. Material changes will be noted in the game's store listing.
