About Us

We make games we’d want to play.

PL Labs is a small, independent studio. We build mobile games and apps with simple controls, a strong mood, and enough depth to keep you coming back.

Our first game, Soul Rush, is a one-tap survival runner for Android. It started as one idea: how much tension can you get out of a single tap? Everything in it, from the art to the sound to the difficulty curve, was made in-house.

We’re now working on our next game, Soul Blocks, a quiet block puzzle about setting souls free. Every project we release will live on this site, so this is the place to see what we’re up to.
