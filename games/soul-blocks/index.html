SOUL BLOCKS

Place the stones. Clear the lines.
Set the souls free.

Every stone on the altar holds a soul. Fill a row or a column and the stones break open, and the souls inside rise up and go free. A quiet block puzzle made for one thumb and a few minutes: no timer unless you want one, and no rush.

How it plays

Simple to learn. Deep enough to stay.

Place

Three pieces wait in the tray. Drag each one onto the 8×8 board wherever it fits. When all three are down, three more are dealt.

Clear

Fill a whole row or column and it breaks. Clear several at once, or keep clearing move after move, and your streak multiplies the score.

Survive

The run ends when nothing in the tray fits. Leave space, plan ahead, and don’t let the board close in on you.

The rites

Free enough souls, and the board answers.

  • Grace

    Fills only the empty cells beneath it and can be set down anywhere it fits, however full the board is.

  • The Toll

    Rings once, and every soul on the board goes free. It pays for all of them, and your streak survives.

  • Angry Soul

    Burns out the nine cells around where it lands. It pays nothing, but it can dig you out of a corner.

  • Settling

    Tips the board toward the nearest edge. Stones slide, whatever closes goes free, and the rest falls again.

Rites are paid for in souls. Every one you free is banked toward the next. The Toll can’t be bought: it simply comes, and only to a run that is already going well.

Three ways to play

Modes

Play

Endless

The classic game. No clock, no limit. Chase your best score, and if the board locks up, you’re offered a rescue.

Against time

The Candle

A flame burns while you play, and every line you clear feeds it. The run ends when the candle goes out.

Once a day

Daily Rite

One board a day, the same for everyone, with souls already bound in its stones. Free them all in 21 pieces. One attempt.
